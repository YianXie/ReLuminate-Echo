ReLuminate Echo

A beacon hunt you play with your ears. Headphones on, monitor off.

Put on headphones, then press any key to begin.

Arena

For viewers, not for players. The game is fully winnable with the monitor off.

How to play

  1. Put on headphones. Any stereo pair. Speakers will not work.
  2. Press any key, or tap Start on a touch screen. The game introduces itself and checks your headphones. Press Space at any point to skip ahead.
  3. Press Space for a short practice: two beacons, no clock and no hazards. Or press T to go straight to a timed round.
  4. After practice, press Space to start a round. You get 60 seconds.
  5. Turn with ← and → until you hear a dry click. That click means the beacon is dead ahead.
  6. Hold ↑ to walk. The beacon pulses faster as you close on it and slower if you are going the wrong way.
  7. When the two rising notes play you are in range. Press Enter to collect it and a new beacon appears somewhere else.
  8. Avoid the low hums. Walking into one costs you five seconds.

Controls

  • ← / → — turn left and right
  • ↑ — walk forward
  • Space — sonar ping (also starts practice from the introduction, and a timed round after that)
  • Enter — collect the beacon when you are on it
  • Esc — pause and hear your score (in practice, end the practice)
  • P — start a practice round, before or between rounds
  • T — start a timed round, before or between rounds
  • H — repeat the controls

On a phone or tablet

  • The controls sit at the bottom of the screen, in three rows. Bottom row, left to right: Turn left, Walk, Turn right. Hold them down to keep going; a quick tap moves a little.
  • Middle row: Ping on the left, Collect on the right.
  • Top row: Practice, Timed round, Pause and Help.
  • With VoiceOver or TalkBack on, double-tap and hold a movement button to keep turning or walking, and turn the game's own speech off below if you hear everything twice.
  • The game keeps the screen awake while it is open, and locking the phone pauses it. To play with the display dark, use VoiceOver's Screen Curtain (three-finger triple-tap).

Settings

  • S — speech on or off
  • - / = — volume down and up
  • C — high contrast visuals
  • D — download this session's data as JSON

What you are hearing

Sound What it means
Pulsing clear tone The beacon. Its direction is where it sounds like it is; the pulse rate rises from twice a second across the arena to eight times a second on top of it.
Short dry click You are aimed at the beacon. It clicks the moment you come on line, and about once a second while you stay there. Walk forward.
Two rising notes Close enough to collect. Press Enter.
Three rising notes Collected. Your score went up and a new beacon has appeared.
Low buzzing hum A hazard. Steer around it.
Low thud You hit a hazard. Five seconds gone.
Soft hiss A wall. You are running out of arena.
Rising whoosh Your sonar ping. Everything nearby answers once.

If you are struggling

  • Sweep, do not stare. Turning is the skill. Headphone audio is far better at left versus right than at front versus back, so keep rotating rather than standing still trying to judge.
  • Trust the click over the tone. It is a yes-or-no signal, so you never have to judge a small angle by ear.
  • If the pulses slow down, turn around. You are walking away from it.
  • Lost? Press Space for a ping, or H to hear the controls again.

Everything you hear is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API. There are no sound files. Direction comes from binaural rendering, so ordinary stereo headphones are all you need — speakers will not work.
