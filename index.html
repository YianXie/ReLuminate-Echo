ReLuminate Echo

A beacon hunt you play with your ears. Headphones on, monitor off.

Put on headphones, then press any key to begin.

Controls

  • ← / → — turn left and right
  • ↑ — walk forward
  • Space — sonar ping (and start the next round)
  • Enter — collect the beacon when you are on it
  • Esc — pause and hear your score
  • H — repeat the controls

Everything you hear is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API. There are no sound files. Direction comes from binaural rendering, so ordinary stereo headphones are all you need — speakers will not work.
